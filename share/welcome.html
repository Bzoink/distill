Distill

Distill is a content-first browser. It fetches a page, runs that page's own scripts, takes the part worth reading, and lays it out again in its own reader. One typeface, one column, one set of themes — whatever site the words came from.

That is the whole idea. A page arrives as somebody else's layout; what you wanted was the article. Distill keeps the article, the headings, the tables, the forms and the pictures, and leaves the rest of the page behind.

Reading

Type a web address in the bar above, or type words and Distill will search for them. dn.se is somewhere to go; kingfisher hunting is something to look for.

  • Back and forward are the arrows at the top left.
  • Zoom with Ctrl and plus or minus; Ctrl and 0 returns to 100%. The page grows with the window between comfortable limits.
  • Jump to the images, tables, forms, videos, results or links on a long page with Alt and a number from 0 to 6, or use the list button.

Taking text with you

  • Drag across the text to select it. Double-click takes a word, triple-click takes a paragraph, Ctrl and A takes the page.
  • Ctrl and C copies. What you paste is the prose put back together — a line that only wrapped because the column is this wide arrives as one sentence.

Pictures

Images are listed rather than loaded, so a page costs what its words cost. Open one by clicking it; Escape closes it again, and the arrow keys step through the rest of the page's pictures.

Settings

The button at the top right holds the reader's theme — warm, night or paper — the zoom level, and the page Distill opens with. To make the page you are reading the one it starts with, choose Start page and then Use the current page.

What Distill does not do

It does not reproduce a site's own design, and it is not trying to. It plays no video or audio. It will tell you when a page is a bot challenge or a paywall rather than pretending it got through one. Some pages are built so that there is nothing to extract, and Distill will say so instead of showing you an empty frame.

When a page defeats it, the button in Settings hands the address to your ordinary browser.

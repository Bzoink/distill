State the reader does not read

This page ships its content twice: once as an assignment-style state blob that no adapter understands, and once as the prose you are reading. The survey exists to record the first kind without pretending to use it.

The blob is written by script, so it cannot be seen before the page has run, and it is an ordinary inline script rather than a JSON-typed one, which is why the adapter path never looks at it at all.

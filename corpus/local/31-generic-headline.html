Tug boats return to the inner harbour

The two tugs withdrawn from the inner harbour in June returned to service on Tuesday morning, the port authority said in a statement.

Dredging in the eastern channel finished ahead of schedule, restoring the draught the larger tugs need at low water.

A third tug remains laid up pending an engine survey, while crews prepare the navigation lights for the winter season.

Ferry timetable consultation closes on Friday

Responses to the winter timetable consultation must reach the operator by Friday afternoon.

Two of the three proposed options drop the late sailing.

The operator is collecting feedback from passengers and will publish the final timetable after the consultation closes.

Harbour master reports a quiet weekend

No incidents were logged over the weekend despite the swell warning that was in force from Saturday evening until Sunday lunchtime.

The harbour office will publish another notice if conditions change.

Crews completed routine checks on the pontoons and kept the channel open throughout the afternoon.

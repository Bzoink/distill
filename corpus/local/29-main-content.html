Tug boats return to the inner harbour

The two tugs that were withdrawn from the inner harbour in June went back into service on Tuesday morning, the port authority said in a statement.

What changed

Dredging in the eastern channel finished a fortnight ahead of schedule, which restores the draught the larger tugs need at low water.

A third tug remains laid up pending an engine survey.

Ferry timetable consultation closes on Friday

Responses to the winter timetable consultation must reach the operator by Friday afternoon. Two of the three proposed options drop the late sailing.

Crane hire costs rise for the second quarter running

The mobile crane tariff goes up by four percent from October, the second rise this year, which the operator attributes to insurance and fuel.

Pilot boat crew training moves to the new simulator

Fishing co-operative signs a new landing agreement

The inshore fishing co-operative has signed a three-year agreement for landing rights at the west quay, replacing the annual renewal it had.

Birds of the harbour

Birds return each morning to the harbour. Their numbers vary with the tides and the weather along the coast, but when the fog lifts the first groups usually gather on the breakwater before moving to the inner docks.

The local wildlife survey counted nearly two hundred birds along the old wall after high tide, and a further hundred inland. Its volunteers have kept the same route for years so the changes in each season can be compared with earlier observations.

The fields first became a sanctuary in the nineteen-seventies, when the harbour authority persuaded the town to limit traffic during the nesting season. Ferries still run along the channel but crews keep their distance from the breakwater and its nests.
